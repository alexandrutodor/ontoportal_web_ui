OntoPortal Web Components

Native, zero-dependency Custom Elements with ARIA accessibility and shadow DOM encapsulation.

1. Form Autocomplete (<ontoportal-autocomplete>)

Accessible combobox with asynchronous search, keyword highlighting, and form integration.

<script src="https://stage.matportal.org/widgets/modern/ontoportal-widgets.js"></script>
<ontoportal-autocomplete
  name="class_uri"
  api-url="https://data.stage.matportal.org"
  ontologies="CHMO"
  placeholder="Search...">
</ontoportal-autocomplete>

<ontoportal-tree ontology="CHMO" searchable="true"></ontoportal-tree>
